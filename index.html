How to prep for pickup day

Five minutes of prep makes the pickup twice as fast.

1

Pile it in one spot

Garage, driveway, or curbside. One pile means one trip in and out.

2

Keep a "don't take" zone

Anything staying put goes in a separate corner or room. Label it if it's close to the pile.

3

Clear a path

Move cars and unlock gates so the truck can get close. Every 20 feet of carry adds time.

4

Text us a photo

A quick photo of the pile gets you a firm price before we roll — no guesswork on arrival.
