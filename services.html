A note on pricing

These are real ranges, not teaser rates. The final number is confirmed on arrival before we lift anything, and it does not change mid-job. If the load is smaller than you thought, you pay less — we price what we actually haul.

What we take — and what we can't

✓ We'll haul it

  • Furniture, mattresses, couches
  • Appliances (fridge, stove, washer, dryer)
  • Electronics and e-waste
  • Scrap metal of all kinds
  • Renovation debris, drywall, lumber
  • Yard waste, brush, branches
  • Hot tubs (cut up on site)
  • Estate and hoarding cleanouts

✗ We can't take

  • Paint, chemicals, solvents
  • Asbestos-containing materials
  • Fuel tanks and propane tanks
  • Wet/moldy hazardous waste

Not sure? Text us a photo — we'll tell you straight whether we can take it and what it costs.
