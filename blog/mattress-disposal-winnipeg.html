Mattress Disposal in Winnipeg: Cost and Options

By Move & Metal · October 22, 2026 · 3 min read

Mattresses are the most awkward thing to get rid of in Winnipeg. Too big for the garbage, banned from most donation piles once they are stained, and the dump charges a fee for them. Here are your real options.

Your options, cheapest first

  1. Give it away. A clean mattress on a buy-and-sell group goes fast. Stained or sagging, it will sit there.
  2. Haul it to the dump yourself. You pay the disposal fee plus your time, fuel, and a borrowed truck.
  3. Book a pickup. We come to you, carry it out, and pay the dump fee. This is the whole job for most people.

What a pickup costs

JobStarting atNotes

The dump charges a disposal fee on every mattress, and we pass that fee through at cost, never marked up. It is baked into the quote, not added after.

How to make it fast

  • Text three photos: a wide shot of the room, a close-up of the mattress, and the path to the door. That gets you a firm price in one text instead of a range.
  • Strip the bedding. We take the mattress and frame, not the sheets.
  • Clear the path. Third-floor walkup with a narrow stairwell takes longer. A photo of the stairs keeps the quote honest.

Winnipeg-specific tip: winter garage and shed pickups are common here. A mattress stored in an unheated garage can freeze to the floor. Send a photo of the access too, like the iced-over side gate, so the quote stays honest.

What we never do

  • No surprise fees added after the quote. The number on arrival matches the text quote or you send us home.
  • No dumping it in a back lane. Everything goes to the transfer station, and we sweep up before we leave.
