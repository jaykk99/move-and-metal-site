Free Scrap Metal Pickup in Winnipeg: What Qualifies

By Move & Metal · October 22, 2026 · 3 min read

Short answer: if the load is all metal, the pickup is free. We make our money at the scrap yard, so you pay nothing. Here is exactly what counts.

What qualifies for free pickup

Free pickup

  • Appliances: fridges, stoves, washers, dryers, dishwashers
  • BBQs, filing cabinets, metal shelving
  • Exercise equipment with metal frames
  • Wire, copper pipe, eavestrough
  • Auto parts: rotors, panels, rims, exhaust
  • Shop scrap and offcuts

Not free (priced as haul-away)

  • Mixed loads: metal buried in household junk
  • Mostly-wood or mostly-plastic items
  • Items with Freon still in the lines
  • Anything we cannot lift safely in one visit

The rule is simple: all-metal loads are free, mixed loads are priced as junk haul-away (starting at $49). If you are not sure which side your pile falls on, text a photo. We will tell you straight before we come.

How it works

  • Text a photo of the pile to 204-972-0325. One photo is usually enough.
  • We confirm free or priced before booking. No surprises on arrival.
  • Curbside or in-home pickup. You do not need to be there for curbside.
  • We sweep up after every job, free or not.

Shop owners: we do recurring scrap pickups for auto and machine shops on your schedule. Rotors, panels, rims, exhaust, wire, offcuts. One text sets it up, then we just show up when the pile is ready.

Why free? What is the catch?

No catch. Scrap yards pay by weight for sorted metal, and that covers the trip. The only thing that breaks the deal is a mixed load, because then we are paying dump fees on the junk half. Separate the metal from the junk and the metal half rides free.
