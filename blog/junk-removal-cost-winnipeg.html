How Much Does Junk Removal Cost in Winnipeg?

By Move & Metal · October 21, 2026 · 4 min read

Short answer: most Winnipeg junk removal jobs land between $99 and $249. Here is the real breakdown, no fine print.

These are starting prices. The final number gets confirmed on arrival, before we lift anything. If it does not match what we quoted, you say no and we leave. That is the deal.

What actually drives the price

It is not the weight. It is three things:

  • Volume. How much truck space it takes. A half load is a half load whether it is pillows or concrete blocks.
  • Labour. Third-floor walkup with no elevator costs more in time than curbside. Stairs, tight hallways, and long carries add up.
  • Dump fees. Mattresses, tires, and electronics carry disposal fees at the dump. We pass those through at cost, never marked up.

How to keep the number low

  • Pile it in one spot. If everything is in the garage instead of scattered through the house, the job goes faster and stays at the quoted load size.
  • Separate the scrap. Metal appliances, wire, and copper can qualify for free scrap pickup. A mixed pile costs you more.
  • Send photos first. Three photos (wide shot, close-up, path to the door) get you an accurate quote in one text instead of a range.
  • Skip the bag-by-bag pricing. Some companies charge per bag or per item and the total creeps. Load-based pricing is one number.

Winnipeg-specific tip: winter pickups from unheated garages and sheds are common here. Frozen items can stick and take longer, so send a photo of access too, like the iced-over side gate. It keeps the quote honest.

What we never do

  • No surprise fees added after the quote.
  • No charging by the hour for junk hauling (moving help is the only hourly service, at $60/hr per mover).
  • No bait pricing. The number on arrival matches the text quote or you send us home.
